How to recognise genuine leather

Simple checks you can do at home. Our products are genuine leather.

Water drop

Genuine leather has tiny pores, so a small drop of water slowly soaks in. On plastic or synthetic leather the drop beads up and rolls off.

Check the edge

A cut edge of genuine leather looks fibrous. Synthetic leather has a smooth edge that looks like fabric or plastic.

Press with your hand

Genuine leather wrinkles slightly when pressed and shows a natural, uneven grain. Synthetic leather has a grain that is identical everywhere.
