The Authenticity Guarantee

How to verify genuine Pakistani hide vs cheap synthetic Rexine.

1. The Water Drop Test

Real top-grain leather absorbs a tiny drop of water within 30-60 seconds due to natural pores. Synthetic Rexine causes water to bead up and roll off completely.

2. Flame Resistance

Genuine cowhide naturally resists brief exposure to a lighter flame without melting or producing chemical plastic smoke. Fake leather melts instantly.

3. Scratch & Healing

Fingernail scratches on our rich leather can be easily buffed out using warm thumb pressure due to natural oils embedded inside cow leather.
