Asli leather ki pehchaan

Ghar par karne wale aam checks. Hamara maal genuine leather hai.

Pani ka qatra

Asli leather mein chhote pores hote hain, pani ka halka qatra kuch der mein jazb ho jata hai. Plastic/rexine par qatra gol ho kar phisal jata hai.

Kinara dekhein

Asli leather ke kate hue kinare par reshedar (fibre) sa nazar aata hai. Synthetic ka kinara saaf, kapray ya plastic jaisa hota hai.

Haath se dabayein

Asli leather dabane par halki jhurriyan banata hai aur natural, ghair yaksan grain dikhata hai. Synthetic par grain har jagah bilkul ek jaisa hota hai.
